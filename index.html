Questions,answered.

Is Handbill free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited estimates and clients and 3 invoices. Handbill Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year (the yearly plan starts with a free week) and unlocks unlimited invoices, your logo on the PDF, no Handbill footer, and overdue reminders.

Does Handbill take a cut of my payments?

No. Handbill never processes payments. Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or card through your own reader, and you mark the invoice paid. There are no payment fees.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account and no sign-up. Handbill works offline, so you can write an estimate in a basement or on a roof. Your data stays on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backup.

Which trades is Handbill for?

Any solo trade or small service business. On first launch you pick your trade, such as electrician, plumber, HVAC, handyman, house cleaning, lawn care, landscaping, painting, pressure washing, roofing, carpentry, flooring, pool service, appliance repair, pest control, window cleaning, junk removal or mobile mechanic, and Handbill fills in the common jobs, units and wording.

Can I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. When the client says yes, one tap turns the estimate into an invoice with every line, tax and discount copied over.

How do I send an invoice?

Handbill makes a clean PDF with your business details. Send it by email, text message or any app from the share sheet. Paid invoices work as a receipt.

Does it work on Android or the web?

Handbill is an iPhone app for iOS 17 and later. There is no Android or web version.
