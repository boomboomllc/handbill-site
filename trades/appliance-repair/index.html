Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "replaced the dryer belt and idler pulley, cleaned the vent, 1 hr labor". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for appliance repair

  • Write the model and serial number in the line detail. Part lookups, warranty claims and repeat calls go faster.
  • Say up front whether the service call is credited toward the repair, and show it on the invoice either way.
  • When a part has to be ordered, send an estimate with the part and the return trip, and take the part cost as a deposit.
