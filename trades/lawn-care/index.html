Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "mow and blow, edged the beds and put down 3 yards of mulch". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for lawn care

  • Bill recurring mowing once a month: one invoice per customer with each visit date on its own line.
  • Put your minimum charge on every estimate so small yards still cover the stop.
  • Price mulch per yard installed and write the color on the line, so there is no surprise on site.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for lawn care?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for lawn care?

When you pick Lawn care on first launch, Handbill adds 46 common jobs in groups like Mowing, Lawn treatments, Cleanups, the units you bill in, and payment terms due in 15 days. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
