Tips for pest control

  • Write the next service date in the notes so the customer knows when you will be back.
  • Quote the initial service and the quarterly plan on one estimate, so the customer sees the whole year at once.
  • If your state asks for it on the service record, add the product name and EPA registration number to the line detail.
