Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "initial service, set 4 bait stations and knocked down 2 wasp nests". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for pest control

  • Write the next service date in the notes so the customer knows when you will be back.
  • Quote the initial service and the quarterly plan on one estimate, so the customer sees the whole year at once.
  • If your state asks for it on the service record, add the product name and EPA registration number to the line detail.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for pest control?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for pest control?

When you pick Pest control on first launch, Handbill adds 43 common jobs in groups like Service plans, Insects, Bed bugs, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
