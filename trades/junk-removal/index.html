Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "half a load of junk, 2 mattresses and a fridge, carried down 1 flight of stairs". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for junk removal

  • Quote by how much of the truck the job fills and write the share on the line, such as 0.5 load. A number the customer can see in the truck is easy to agree on.
  • Put items with their own disposal cost, such as mattresses, tires and fridges, on separate lines so the fee is clear before you load.
  • Take a photo of the pile before you start. If the volume is questioned later, you have it.
