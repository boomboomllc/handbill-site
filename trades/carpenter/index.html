Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "hung 2 interior doors, ran 80 ft of baseboard and replaced 6 deck boards". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for carpenters

  • Price trim by the linear foot and doors by the opening; customers can compare those numbers.
  • Before opening a wall or deck, write a line for rot with your price per board or sheet.
  • Say in the line detail who supplies the doors and lumber, so a materials price change is clear.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for carpenters?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for carpenters?

When you pick Carpentry on first launch, Handbill adds 49 common jobs in groups like Framing, Trim and finish, Doors and windows, the units you bill in, and payment terms due in 7 days. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
