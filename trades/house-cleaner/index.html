Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "deep clean 3 bed 2 bath, inside oven and fridge". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for house cleaners

  • Price the first clean on its own line; recurring visits are easier to keep when the first one is priced right.
  • Put each extra the client asked for, like the oven or the fridge, on its own line so they see what it costs.
  • Write your cancellation and lockout fees in the default terms once, so they are on every estimate.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for house cleaners?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for house cleaners?

When you pick House cleaning on first launch, Handbill adds 40 common jobs in groups like Cleans, Rooms, Add-ons, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
