Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "front pads and rotors, replaced the battery and 1.5 hrs labor". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for mobile mechanics

  • Put the year, make, model and mileage in the job field so every invoice shows which vehicle you worked on.
  • Bill the diagnostic fee as its own line. If the customer goes ahead with the repair, you can lower it on the invoice.
  • Ask for a parts deposit before ordering expensive parts, so a cancelled job does not leave you holding them.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for mobile mechanics?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for mobile mechanics?

When you pick Mobile mechanic on first launch, Handbill adds 50 common jobs in groups like Service and labor, Maintenance, Fluids, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
