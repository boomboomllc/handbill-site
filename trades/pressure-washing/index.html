Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "soft washed the house, 1200 sq ft driveway and the back patio". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for pressure washing

  • Set a minimum charge, so a small job still covers the drive and setup.
  • Keep each surface on its own line with its square feet, then next year's repeat is one tap.
  • Note on the estimate that rust and old oil stains may not fully lift, so it is agreed before you start.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for pressure washing?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for pressure washing?

When you pick Pressure washing on first launch, Handbill adds 39 common jobs in groups like House and roof, Flatwork, Stains and treatments, the units you bill in, and payment terms due in 7 days. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
