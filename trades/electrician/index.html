Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "added 3 GFCIs, swapped a ceiling fan and pulled a permit". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

An invoice that fits the job

With Pro, pick the Work order style: boxed fields, a gridded table and a line for the customer to sign, like the work orders electricians already fill out. Add your logo, cropped to a circle if you like, and pick from ten colors. Seven other styles are there too. See every style.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for electricians

  • Price your common jobs once with parts included, so a GFCI or a fan swap is one tap on site.
  • Keep the permit on its own line at cost, so the customer sees what goes to the city.
  • Found a code problem outside the job? Add it as a separate estimate so the customer approves it in writing.
