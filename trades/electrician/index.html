Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "added 3 GFCIs, swapped a ceiling fan and pulled a permit". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for electricians

  • Price your common jobs once with parts included, so a GFCI or a fan swap is one tap on site.
  • Keep the permit on its own line at cost, so the customer sees what goes to the city.
  • Found a code problem outside the job? Add it as a separate estimate so the customer approves it in writing.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for electricians?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for electricians?

When you pick Electrician on first launch, Handbill adds 50 common jobs in groups like Service and labor, Outlets and switches, Lighting and fans, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
