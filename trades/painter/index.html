Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "2 bedrooms, ceilings and trim, patched 3 holes". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for painters

  • Write the paint brand, color code and sheen on the invoice so touch-ups match later.
  • Price walls, ceilings and trim as separate lines, so the customer sees each part and can drop one.
  • Put heavy patching or peeling paint on its own line, so extra prep is agreed before you start.
