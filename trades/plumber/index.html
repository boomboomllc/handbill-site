Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "snaked the kitchen drain, replaced 2 angle stops and 1 hr labor". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for plumbers

  • Put a service call line on every visit, even for a quick fix. It pays for the drive and the diagnosis.
  • On bigger jobs, write two estimates, such as repair and replace, so the customer chooses instead of saying no.
  • Add the model and serial number to the line detail when you install a water heater or pump. Warranty claims go faster.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for plumbers?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for plumbers?

When you pick Plumber on first launch, Handbill adds 50 common jobs in groups like Service call and labor, Drains and sewer, Fixtures, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
