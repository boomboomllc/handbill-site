Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "6 yards of mulch, planted 4 shrubs and edged the beds". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for landscapers

  • Take a deposit before you order plants and stone, so materials are paid up front.
  • Write the plant warranty and its watering terms on every estimate, so a lost shrub is not a dispute later.
  • Price mulch, soil and rock by the yard or ton installed, and put delivery on its own line.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for landscapers?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for landscapers?

When you pick Landscaping on first launch, Handbill adds 49 common jobs in groups like Design and prep, Planting, Mulch and rock, the units you bill in, and payment terms due in 7 days. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
