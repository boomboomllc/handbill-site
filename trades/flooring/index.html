Tips for flooring and tile installers

  • Add 10 percent waste to the material line, and more for diagonal tile or herringbone.
  • Put leveling and subfloor repair on the estimate at a square foot price, so surprises after demo are agreed.
  • Write the product, color and lot number in the line detail; a matching repair later depends on it.
