Say what you did

Type the job the way you'd say it, for example "mounted a TV, patched 2 holes and recaulked the tub, 3 hours labor". Handbill turns it into invoice lines priced with your own rates. You check them and send.

Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for handymen

  • Charge a minimum per visit so a 20 minute fix still pays for the drive.
  • Put each task from a honey-do list on its own line; customers approve faster when they can see each one.
  • Write who supplied the parts in the line detail, yours or the customer's, so warranty questions are easy later.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for handymen?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for handymen?

When you pick Handyman on first launch, Handbill adds 49 common jobs in groups like Time and visits, Mounting and hanging, Assembly, the units you bill in, and payment terms due on receipt. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
