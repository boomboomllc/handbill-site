Get paid without fees

Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader. Handbill records the payment and never takes a cut. Overdue invoices are flagged so you know who to chase.

Tips for pool service

  • Bill chemicals beyond the routine weekly dose as their own lines. A green pool or a storm week should not come out of your monthly rate.
  • Write the readings, such as chlorine, pH and alkalinity, in the line detail on openings and cleanups. It answers most questions before they are asked.
  • Send monthly service invoices on the same day each month. Customers pay on a routine, and you see quickly who is behind.

Questions

Is there a free invoice app for pool service?

Yes. Handbill is free to start on iPhone with unlimited estimates and 3 invoices. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year for unlimited invoices, your logo and overdue reminders. It never takes a fee on payments.

What does Handbill set up for pool service?

When you pick Pool service on first launch, Handbill adds 49 common jobs in groups like Service visits, Openings and cleanups, Chemicals, the units you bill in, and payment terms due in 10 days. You add your own prices once and reuse them.

Can I write an estimate on site without signal?

Yes. Handbill works offline with no account. Write the estimate on the job, then send it as a PDF by text or email when you are ready.
