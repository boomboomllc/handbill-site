"Free" invoice apps aren't free

Square and Wave don't charge for invoices because they make their money on your payments: about 3% of every card payment, on every job, forever.

On a $1,000 job paid by card, that's about $30 gone. Bill $40,000 a year by card and that's about $1,200 gone in fees. Handbill Pro is $49.99 a year, and your clients' money goes straight to you.

Why trades pick Handbill

Questions

What is the cheapest invoice app for contractors?

Handbill. It is free to start, and $49.99 a year gets unlimited invoices with no fees on payments. Apps that call themselves free, like Square Invoices and Wave, take about 3% of every card payment, so a business billing $40,000 a year by card pays well over $1,000 in fees.

Which invoice app has no payment fees?

Handbill. It never touches your money, so it can never take a cut. Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader, and you mark the invoice paid in one tap.

Is Handbill better than Joist or Invoice Simple for a one-person business?

For a solo tradesperson, yes. Handbill costs less than either for unlimited invoices, sets itself up for your trade on day one, works offline without an account, and keeps your data on your phone.
