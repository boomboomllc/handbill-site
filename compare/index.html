Where Handbill wins

  • No payment fees, ever. On a $2,000 job, a 2.9% card fee is $58. Handbill doesn't touch the money.
  • Set up for your trade. Pick from 19 trades and start with that trade's jobs and units. See the trades.
  • Works offline, no account. Your data stays on your phone.
  • Simple pricing. One Pro plan, unlimited invoices, $49.99 a year.

Where others win

  • Clients can pay by card from a link in the invoice.
  • Web and Android apps, and team accounts.
  • Bookkeeping, scheduling and job management tools.

Questions

What is the cheapest invoice app for contractors?

For unlimited invoices on iPhone, Handbill Pro is $49.99 a year, with a free plan to start. Square Invoices and Wave are free but take card fees on payments. Invoice Simple and Joist cost more per month for unlimited documents but add online payments and more tools.

Which invoice app has no payment fees?

Handbill never processes payments, so it takes no fees. Clients pay you directly by cash, check, bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo or your own card reader, and you mark the invoice paid.

When is another app a better choice than Handbill?

Pick Invoice Simple, Joist or Square if you want clients to pay by card from a link in the invoice, need a web or Android app, or have a team sharing one account. Handbill is iPhone only and built for one person.
